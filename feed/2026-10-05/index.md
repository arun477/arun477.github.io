# OpenAI extends content provenance to text for the EU

OpenAI begins labelling text provenance to meet EU rules, while flagging how limited current text watermarking is.


- [OpenAI extends content provenance to text under EU rules](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107164650249101695) (OpenAI, news, Policy): First major lab to apply provenance to generated text for EU compliance, while openly noting text marks are easy to strip. Sets a template others will face.
