# DeepMind's Genie 4 world model; Anthropic moves into drug discovery

Google DeepMind shows Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model. Anthropic shares lessons from six drug-discovery partnerships, Google Research proposes a sleep phase for continual learning, and LangChain details how it built its design agent.


- [Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2115114028338962672) (Google DeepMind, release): DeepMind's newest world model builds photorealistic scenes you explore and steer with text in real time, with sound, physics and characters that speak. Trusted testers get it first; world models like this are becoming training grounds for agents and robots.
- [Anthropic details its drug-discovery partnerships and starts its own drug research](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2115178675050307942) (Anthropic, news): Anthropic scientists spent months partnering with six drug developers and have started drug discovery of their own. The post shares where Claude actually helps across the stages of finding a new medicine.
- [Google Research's sleep framework lets LLMs keep learning after deployment](https://research.google/blog/the-sleep-framework-a-new-way-for-llms-to-retain-knowledge-in-continual-learning/) (Google Research, paper): Models alternate between active use and an offline sleep phase that writes short-term context into reserved MoE memory blocks. Google reports new facts retained, long-context recall up to 10M tokens and better reasoning without catastrophic forgetting.
- [How LangChain built Content Studio, its in-house design agent](https://www.langchain.com/blog/design-agent) (LangChain, blog): Build log of an agent that makes videos, graphics and decks from a Slack prompt: a sandbox per thread as a designer's computer, design principles as context, rules deleted as models improve, and long async runs with approvals. Harness is open source.
