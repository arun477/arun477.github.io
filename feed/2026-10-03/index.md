# The case for default hard budget caps

A quiet day for releases. Simon Willison argued that usage-billed services, especially AI APIs, need hard spending caps on by default.


- [We are going to need default hard budget caps on pretty much everything](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/3/default-hard-budget-caps/) (Simon Willison, blog, Agents): As agents call paid APIs on their own, runaway bills become a real risk. Willison makes the case that hard spending limits should be on by default.
