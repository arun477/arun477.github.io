# Anthropic opens its IPO with $17B quarterly revenue

Anthropic launches its public offering and shows its books for the first time: $17.0B Q3 revenue, up more than 12x year over year.


- **Spike.** [Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition) (NVIDIA, news, Big Tech, 22:30 UTC): Nvidia's board says Huang has left as CEO and board member effective immediately, with Kress as interim CEO and Harvey Jones as the company's first board chair. Nvidia says its product roadmap and customer commitments continue unchanged, and Q3 results still land on Nov 17.
- [Anthropic launches its IPO roadshow with $17B quarterly revenue](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2120841731302154708) (Anthropic, news, Labs, 13:44 UTC): Anthropic opened its public offering with a prospectus showing $17.0B revenue in Q3 2026, more than 12x a year earlier, and over 11,900 customers paying $100K+ a year.
- [Google DeepMind releases Gemini 4 Hydrogen, its fast everyday model](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120940338869055788) (Google DeepMind, release, Models, 22:30 UTC): Google says Hydrogen finishes tasks 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and beats Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, with better instruction following and tool calls. Cursor reports 54.3% on CursorBench at default effort, up from 39.6% for 3.8 Flash.
- [Anthropic launches Claude Meetings, Claude joins your team's video calls](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2120973994191094179) (Anthropic, news, Agents, 22:30 UTC): Claude can now sit in a team video call and build docs, whiteboards and other artifacts while people are still talking. It is rolling out in beta on Team and Enterprise plans.
- [Thinking Machines opens Inkling 2 Small weights under Apache 2.0](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2120982625582195011) (Thinking Machines, release, Models, 22:30 UTC): The 594B-total, 17B-active MoE for agents and coding, launched last week, can now be downloaded from Hugging Face under a permissive Apache 2.0 license, so teams can self-host and fine-tune it.
- [World Labs opens its Atlas world model to everyone, with an API](https://x.com/theworldlabs/status/2120942033401717108) (World Labs, release, Models, 22:30 UTC): Anyone can now use Atlas through World Labs' apps and API endpoints to turn an idea into a 3D world, then export keyframes, video clips or 3D Gaussian splats.
- [Grok Bot on Mac can now use Messages, Mail, Notes, Calendar and Contacts](https://x.com/bot/status/2120946968403095906) (Grok Bot, news, Agents, 22:30 UTC): The personal agent's Mac app now works directly with Apple's built-in apps, including Messages, Mail, Notes, Reminders, Calendar, Location and Contacts, so it can act on your own data rather than only in the cloud.
