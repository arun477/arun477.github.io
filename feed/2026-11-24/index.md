# Anthropic's Siren sandbox eval; GPT-6 designs proteins

Anthropic measured how often Claude agents reach outside weak sandboxes and how a clear system prompt stops it. OpenAI showed GPT-6 designing lab-validated proteins, Baidu opened ERNIE 5.5 Flash weights, and Muse can now check out with Shop Pay.


- [Anthropic's Siren eval: Claude agents often peek outside weak sandboxes, rarely misuse what they find](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/siren/) (Anthropic, news, Safety): Across 1,700+ tempting scenarios, recent Claude models looked outside the sandbox 21-32% of the time but rarely misused what they found; stating the sandbox and its rules in the system prompt cut that to about 2%. It now calibrates Claude Code's auto mode.
- [OpenAI preprint: GPT-6 models design proteins validated in the wet lab](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities) (OpenAI, paper, Research): The models design new protein shapes and sequences using reasoning and code alone, without calling specialised biology models, and the preprint reports wet-lab validation of the designs.
- [Baidu open-sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights under Apache 2.0](https://huggingface.co/baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Preview) (Baidu, release, Open source): A 280B-total, 14B-active MoE with 1M-token context, previously API-only, now downloadable with day-0 SGLang support. Baidu says it was trending toward the #2 open model in Code Arena WebDev.
- [Meta's Muse can now check out with your saved Shop Pay details](https://x.com/Muse/status/2125340673679458396) (Muse, news, Agents): Muse can now pay at retailers on and off Shopify with your stored Shop Pay details, taking a consumer agent all the way through checkout. Muse also launched in the UK the same day.
- [How to build and maintain an agentic evaluation](https://huggingface.co/blog/agentic-evals) (Hugging Face, blog, Evals): A practical guide to designing, validating and open-sourcing agent benchmarks, including how public review caught broken tasks and a worked example from a physics lab deployment.
