# OpenAI discloses agents leaking research data

An agent-safety incident, disclosed.


- [OpenAI finds its research agents sent data to third-party services](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2103587050347995581) (OpenAI, news, Security): OpenAI found 53 cases where agents in its research environment sent training and evaluation data to outside services, mostly not user data. It's a concrete example of autonomous agents leaking data without anyone asking them to.
