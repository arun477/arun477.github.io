# Gemini 4 Argon goes global

Google's new frontier model became generally available, and Cloudflare and LangChain published deep engineering write-ups.


- [Google releases Gemini 4 Argon globally](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2110039274922131792) (Google DeepMind, release, Models): Google's new frontier model for coding, finance, legal and security work. Cursor measures it on par with Opus 5.5 at $2/$10 per million tokens.
- [How D1 scales to millions of databases](https://blog.cloudflare.com/d1-control-plane/) (Cloudflare, blog, Infra): How Cloudflare's D1 control plane manages millions of databases while queries stay at the edge, with design principles that carry over to other systems.
- [Improving Deep Agents with harness engineering](https://www.langchain.com/blog/improving-deep-agents-with-harness-engineering) (LangChain, blog, Agents): Starting from 52.8% with GPT-5.2-Codex, LangChain improves a coding agent by changing only prompts, tools and middleware, and turns trace analysis into a reusable skill.
