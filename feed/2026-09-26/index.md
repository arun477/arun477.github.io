# Grok Bot gets access to your finances

Personal agents start handling money.


- [Grok Bot connects to bank, card and investment accounts](https://x.com/bot/status/2103936247995752705) (Grok Bot, release, Agents): A Finance integration lets the personal agent see linked accounts and help manage spending and investments. Giving agents access to money raises the bar for approvals and safety.
