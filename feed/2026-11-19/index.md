# OpenAI measures research acceleration; Anthropic goes public

OpenAI reports agents now complete nearly half of multi-hour research tasks and calls for mandatory reporting of self-improvement indicators. Anthropic priced its IPO and starts trading on Nasdaq. Spotify explains when offline evals can stand in for A/B tests.


- [OpenAI: agents now finish nearly half of 2-4 hour research tasks on their own](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/) (OpenAI, news, Research, 13:36 UTC): Autonomous success on 2-4h research tasks rose from 23% to 46% since March, and median researcher agent spend hit $2,400/day. OpenAI argues labs should be required to publicly report self-improvement indicators.
- [Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share; trading on Nasdaq as ANT starts today](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering) (Anthropic, news, Labs, 13:36 UTC): Anthropic sells about 956M new shares, roughly $79B in fresh capital, and the maker of Claude becomes a public company with the quarterly disclosure duties that come with it.
- [Spotify: when you can ship an AI change on offline evals alone](https://engineering.atspotify.com/2026/11/when-can-you-ship-on-offline-evals-alone/) (Spotify Engineering, blog, Evals, 13:36 UTC): A careful look at skipping A/B tests for LLM features: it only works under strong, drifting assumptions, and judge noise can make a 60% offline-online link look like 24%.
