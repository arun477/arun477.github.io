# Claude Haiku 5.5 and GPT-6 for everyone

Anthropic shipped a much cheaper small model and OpenAI rolled GPT-6 with generated UI out to all ChatGPT users.


- [Anthropic releases Claude Haiku 5.5](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2107894039626277339) (Claude, release, Models): About 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5 and Anthropic's most capable small model, which changes the cost of high-volume agent subtasks.
- [GPT-6 and Intelligent UI roll out to everyone in ChatGPT](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107894997538525580) (OpenAI, news, Models): Free users get GPT-6, and answers can now come back as generated interactive interfaces instead of only text.
- [SynthID Detector opens to everyone](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2107834249680499136) (Google DeepMind, news, Policy): Anyone can check whether images, video or audio came from Google's tools or partners such as OpenAI, a rare cross-company detection service.
- [Putting a transformer inside every card authorization](https://builders.ramp.com/post/sequence-aware-card-fraud) (Ramp Engineering, blog, Infra): Ramp runs a transformer over each cardholder's transaction history on every authorization in real time, ranking fraud 14% better than its old model.
- [Grok Bot can search, read and monitor X](https://x.com/bot/status/2107949161878606089) (Grok Bot, release, Agents): Bots can now watch X for topics and accounts and act on what they find, which makes ongoing monitoring a built-in personal-agent task.
