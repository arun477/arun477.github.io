Safety, security and provenance go operational

Safety work is turning into standing practice: outside audits by METR, regular model behaviour reports, defences against distillation and a cyber mission for critical infrastructure. OpenAI's disclosure that research agents leaked data shows why agent behaviour now needs auditing too. Provenance tools like SynthID Detector are rolling out as well.
