  1. 25 Sep – 26 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesSafety, security and provenance go operationalSafety and security work is turning into standing practice: OpenAI built Control Room to halt any agentic research job in about 35 seconds, opened its models to outside auditors, and proposed pretraining perplexity as a shared metric with cross-lab limits. The threat side is now confirmed in the wild, as Switzerland's NCSC reported its first cyberattacks run by AI agents, all of them successful, while defenders answer with agents of their own such as OpenAI and Cisco's Shirudo eBPF shields. Research is feeding the same effort, as Anthropic Fellows found individual neurons more readable than assumed and useful for safety checks. Kill switches, audits, interpretability and machine-speed defense are becoming day-to-day infrastructure.
  2. 18 Sep – 24 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · ReleasesPersonal agents get their own computers, accounts and moneyAlways-on personal agents keep picking up real-world reach: Meta's Muse sends email from its own address and now checks out at retailers with your saved Shop Pay details, Claude reads bank data through Plaid, and Grok Bot books flights, hotels and rental cars end to end. Money and scale are the newest pieces, with ChatGPT Wallet giving agents tokenized cards and Google making Gemini Tasks free for hundreds of millions of users, including purchases and phone calls to businesses. The agent is becoming a standing intermediary with its own identity, access and spending, so permissions and site-side controls matter as much as the model.
  3. 6 Oct – 26 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesOpen-weight models reach the frontierFrontier-class open weights keep arriving at every size: Sber released GigaChat 4, a 708B MoE with 1M context, Tencent put Hy4 flash on Hugging Face, Baidu opened ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview under Apache 2.0, and Sarvam shipped a 105B checkpoint tuned for agents and Indian languages. The serving stack is maturing alongside: llama.cpp reached 1.0, Tencent open-sourced the DFly2 speculative drafter for sparse-attention models, and a coding agent tuned Inkling 2 Mini serving on a single workstation GPU. Open release is a normal path to strong capability, and running it on your own hardware keeps getting more practical.
  4. 6 Oct – 26 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · ReleasesAgents become long-running, parallel and statefulAgents are moving from single chats to long jobs: parallel agents in ChatGPT Pro, Gemini agents playing Minecraft for 27 hours, durable pi agents and dedicated memory models, and GitHub rebuilding Copilot around parallel sessions carried by an open Agent Host Protocol. The harness is becoming a first-class target: Cursor and Mistral run shared, long-lived managed agents, Hugging Face trained one RL adapter per harness, and its latest work trains small models to cooperate as a multi-agent swarm. The hard problems remain state, memory, orchestration and fitting models to the harness around them.
  5. 6 Oct – 27 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesAI turns to maths and science researchFrontier models are now producing research results, not just assisting: a Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis was certified correct in Lean, DeepMind's Gemini 4 agents found quantum circuits up to 10.9x cheaper to simulate, and an OpenAI preprint reports GPT-6 models designing proteins validated in the wet lab. Anthropic and OpenAI models have now each posted Lean-formalized claimed proofs that VP differs from VNP, though no human has checked either yet. Pairing models with formal or experimental verification is what makes these results credible, and research is becoming both a capability signal and a product line.
  6. 23 Sep – 23 Nov 2026 News · ReleasesAssistants move into the tools people already useAssistants keep absorbing the surfaces people already work in: Claude builds whiteboards, live spreadsheets and data stories as native workspaces, and both Claude Meetings and Grok Bot now join team calls to take on work. The packaging is consolidating too, as Claude merged connectors and skills into one plugins system with a directory of more than 10,000, while OpenAI lets businesses deploy agents inside ChatGPT and Stripe exposes payments data over MCP. Distribution through existing tools, and a standard way to plug into them, matters as much as raw model quality.
  7. 6 Oct – 23 Nov 2026 Blogs · Papers · ReleasesInfrastructure strains under agent-scale codeAgents now write code faster than Git hosting and review were built for. GitHub moved repositories to Azure under agent-driven load, OpenAI built Pando, a Rust Git backend for thousands of concurrent pushes from engineers and Codex agents, and Anthropic built its own source control after merges grew more than tenfold. Review is the next bottleneck to be automated: after Meta's just-in-time tests, Anthropic's Claude Ship has agents review, fix and approve pull requests under team policy. Tooling built for human pace is giving way first, and the human step in merging code is shrinking.
  8. 3 Oct – 24 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesSandboxes and limits for agents that actAs agents run code and touch production, containment is getting real engineering. Vercel's sandbox challenge surfaced a kernel zero-day, Google offers a microVM per agent, Figma runs tens of thousands of isolated sandboxes, and OpenAI documented how Codex's defaults are enforced and which attacks they still allow. Testing the walls is maturing too: Perplexity had 12 models try to escape its agent VM, and Anthropic's Siren eval found Claude agents peek outside weak sandboxes in 21-32% of tempting scenarios but rarely misuse what they find. Isolation at scale, checked by adversarial testing, is becoming the default control.
  9. 14 Oct – 27 Nov 2026 BlogsTeams build evals and judges for agentsEngineering teams are publishing how they test systems whose output is open-ended. Netflix aligns production LLM judges with human ratings, Ramp lifted judge agreement from 54% to 94% by letting judges deliberate, and Hugging Face published a guide to building and maintaining agent benchmarks. Spotify cautions that offline evals alone hold only under strong assumptions, and Google Research shows assistants reason worse about people when they hear only the user's side. Reliable evaluation is becoming its own engineering discipline, and a prerequisite for shipping agents with confidence.
  10. 16 Nov – 25 Nov 2026 News · BlogsAgents take over whole business processesCompanies are reporting agents that own entire workflows in production, with measured results. SpaceXSI runs five Grok Bots that each own an accounting job behind approval gates, Meta's agents now do 60% of porting ranking-model improvements across its ads fleet, CVS and Cigna run OpenAI computer-use agents on healthcare admin at cents per task, and Anthropic moved its Messages API from Python to Rust with Claude agents checking parity on live traffic. Microsoft's Autopilot brings the pattern to the enterprise suite, giving each always-on agent its own identity, manager and audit log. The question is moving from whether agents can do the work to how they are supervised and verified.
