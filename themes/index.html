rising

  1. 25 Sep – 13 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · PapersSafety, security and provenance go operationalSafety and security work is turning into standing practice: OpenAI built Control Room so a monitor can halt any agentic research job in about 35 seconds, confidential compute keeps spreading from OpenAI's Private Inference API to Google's TPU 8tx, and Anthropic open-sourced FlashProve to prove a reply came from committed weights. The threat side is operational too, with Cloudflare now seeing 50+ families of AI pentest frameworks scanning its customers from thousands of IPs, while Anthropic published practical rules for building safety evals that hold up. Kill switches, sealed inference, provenance and sound evals are becoming day-to-day lab and network infrastructure.
  2. 18 Sep – 13 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · ReleasesPersonal agents get their own computers, accounts and moneyAlways-on personal agents keep picking up real-world reach: Meta's Muse can send and receive email from its own address, Claude reads bank and card data through Plaid, and Grok Bot now works with Apple's Messages, Mail, Calendar and Contacts on the Mac. Money is the newest piece, as OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet, built with Amex, Mastercard and Visa, gives agents tokenized cards with a security check on every purchase. Open options are arriving too, with n8n Labs' Nod and OpenCode Browse running agents on the user's own computer and browser. The agent is becoming a standing intermediary with its own identity, access and spending, so permissions and site-side controls matter as much as the model.
  3. 6 Oct – 11 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · ReleasesAgents become long-running, parallel and statefulAgents are moving from single chats to long jobs: parallel agents in ChatGPT Pro, Gemini agents playing Minecraft for 27 hours, durable pi agents and dedicated memory models, and GitHub rebuilding Copilot around parallel agent sessions. The harness is going multiplayer as well: Cursor rebuilt Projects so every teammate and their agents share one workspace, Cursor Cloud Agents scaffold and host live web apps, and Mistral Studio runs long-lived managed agents with approvals and tracing. Anthropic's write-up of agent memory treats offline "dreaming" as the pass that corrects bad notes and preloads the next session. The hard problems remain state, memory, orchestration and coordination around the model.
  4. 22 Sep – 12 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · ReleasesClosed labs ship new flagships and faster tiersClosed labs keep shipping capability, speed and price tiers: Google opened Gemini 4 Carbon to all paying developers and followed with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a fast everyday model it says is 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, while Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 is up to 30% faster and 20% cheaper at the top tier and Alibaba opened Qwen4-Max in preview. Cheap decision models are becoming their own category, with Google's Gemini Decision 1 scoring up to 1,000 questions per input in about 200 ms, while Vercel's AI Gateway shows small decision models already handle 26% of requests. Competition is shifting toward price per task and the workloads agents actually run, from flagship reasoning down to cheap high-volume calls.
  5. 6 Oct – 13 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesOpen-weight models reach the frontierFrontier-scale open weights keep arriving: Thinking Machines put Inkling 2 Small, a 594B/17B MoE tuned for agents and coding, out under Apache 2.0 and then detailed how it trained the model end to end in 4-bit, after Mistral Large 4, Muse Spark Open, Reflection's Beam and Kimi K3.1 Pro. Open release is spreading beyond chat models with Runway's Praxis-1 for robot control, and the local stack is maturing as llama.cpp and ggml reached 1.0 with a stable API and ABI. Open release is a normal path to frontier capability, which tightens the gap for anyone building outside the closed APIs.
  6. 6 Oct – 13 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesAI turns to maths and science researchLabs keep pointing frontier models at research work: OpenAI published maths results and opened GPT-Rosalind to academic labs, Anthropic used Claude to help map a bacterial vault particle, DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 traces variants to the cells where disease unfolds, and Google opened Antigravity's /teamwork multi-agent tools to outside researchers after Gemini 4 workflows produced results in maths, chemistry and kernel optimization. Formal methods are moving into real software, as AlphaProof Code verified security properties of Signal's protocol code, and OpenAI's BioBasicsBench scores agents on raw wet-lab data. Research results are becoming both a capability signal and a product line.
  7. 23 Sep – 13 Nov 2026 News · ReleasesAssistants move into the tools people already useAssistants keep absorbing the surfaces people already work in: Claude now builds whiteboards, live spreadsheets and data stories as native workspaces, and meetings became a contested surface within a day, as Claude Meetings and then Grok Bot started joining team calls to answer questions, take on work and track decisions. Muse Spaces turns a description into a persistent mini-app, and the flow also runs the other way, as OpenAI lets businesses deploy agents inside ChatGPT and Stripe exposes payments data to agents over MCP. Distribution through existing tools matters as much as raw model quality.
  8. 3 Oct – 11 Nov 2026 News · Blogs · Papers · ReleasesSandboxes and limits for agents that actAs agents run code and touch production, containment is getting real engineering. Vercel's sandbox challenge surfaced a kernel zero-day, Codex on Windows moved to a container sandbox, Google offers a microVM per agent, and Figma built Nimbus to run tens of thousands of isolated sandboxes for untrusted code. Testing the walls is maturing too: Perplexity had 12 models try to escape its agent VM and found capture-the-flag setups can steer agents away from the boundary under test, and security-tuned models did no better. Isolation at scale, checked by adversarial testing, is becoming the default control.

steady

  1. 18 Oct – 12 Nov 2026 NewsAnthropic reshapes its governance on the way to an IPOAnthropic has opened its IPO roadshow, with a prospectus showing $17.0B of revenue in Q3 2026, more than 12x a year earlier, and over 11,900 customers paying $100K+ a year. It got there after filing an S-1 to list on Nasdaq as ANT, committing 5% of its shares to a Public Benefit Fund, and handing its Agent Skills standard to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation. A frontier lab going public puts its finances and governance in the open and sets a template for how safety-focused labs raise capital.

fading

  1. 6 Oct – 5 Nov 2026 Blogs · PapersInfrastructure strains under agent-scale codeAgents now write code faster than Git hosting and review were built for. GitHub moved repositories to Azure under agent-driven load and has published the redesign of its Git object storage; OpenAI built Pando, a Rust Git backend for thousands of concurrent pushes from engineers and Codex agents, cutting median push latency by about 80% on a petabyte-scale monorepo. Anthropic built its own source control after merges grew more than tenfold, and teams are answering review load with automation such as Meta's just-in-time tests. Tooling built for human pace is giving way first.
