# AI turns to maths and science research

Labs keep pointing frontier models at research work: OpenAI published maths results and opened GPT-Rosalind to academic labs, Anthropic used Claude to help map the first 3D structure of a bacterial vault particle, and Deep Principle released Hydrogen-1.0, an open-weight science model post-trained for chemistry, materials and life sciences. OpenAI's BioBasicsBench now scores agents on raw wet-lab gels, plates and instrument data from real experiments, so science evaluation is catching up with the research demos. Formal review still matters for the biggest claims, from Yang-Mills working groups to Lean-checked complexity results. Research results are becoming both a capability signal and a product line.

## Evidence

- [OpenAI introduces BioBasicsBench for AI agents on wet-lab analysis](https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science/) (OpenAI, news, 2026-11-06): 100 tasks from real Ligo experiments test whether agents can read raw gels, plates and instrument data, catch errors and refuse unsupported conclusions. GPT-6 Astra scores 66.7% at xhigh vs 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol.
- [Anthropic used Claude to help map the first 3D structure of a bacterial vault particle](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2118073665296228863) (Anthropic, news, 2026-11-04): A concrete case of a frontier model contributing to real structural biology work, from Anthropic's own research team.
- [Deep Principle releases Hydrogen-1.0, an open-weight science model post-trained from GLM-5.3](https://blog.deepprinciple.com/introducing-hydrogen-1.0/) (Deep Principle, release, 2026-11-02): MIT-licensed weights (1M context) for chemistry, materials and life sciences, trained on lab records and agent trajectories. Self-reported 38.3% on FrontierScience-Research vs GPT-6 Astra's 33.3%; not yet independently verified.
- [OpenAI and Anthropic models each claim a refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/378.md) (OpenAI, paper, 2026-10-30): Both labs say internal models independently found a subexponential algorithm for counting 3-SAT solutions on Oct 16, with Lean proofs. Impagliazzo, Paturi and Williams are now leading an open review; the result is asymptotic only, with no practical speedup.
- [Physicists led by David Gross convene to vet an AI result on Yang-Mills theory](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/statement-by-the-yangmills-working-group) (KITP, news, 2026-10-28): KITP formed a working group chaired by Nobel laureate David Gross to vet an AI result that includes a proof that quantum Yang-Mills theory exists, half of a Millennium Prize problem. OpenAI amplified it, so physicists are now formally checking model-made frontier science.
- [Anthropic details its drug-discovery partnerships and starts its own drug research](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2115178675050307942) (Anthropic, news, 2026-10-27): Anthropic scientists spent months partnering with six drug developers and have started drug discovery of their own. The post shares where Claude actually helps across the stages of finding a new medicine.
- [Simons Foundation, OpenAI and Anthropic commit $15M to AI in maths research](https://x.com/SimonsFdn/status/2110740786169979088) (Simons Foundation, news, 2026-10-17): Workshops and free compute to help mathematicians verify and build on AI-generated results, after OpenAI's batch of 700 AI-written papers unsettled the field.
- [Claude Mythos 5 designs more efficient quantum circuits from plain-language instructions](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2111152183240511640) (Anthropic, paper, 2026-10-16): From Anthropic's STEM Fellows: a concrete case of a model doing useful design work in a specialised science domain.
- [Grounding AI agents in biological reality: building blocks for autonomous wet labs](https://research.google/blog/grounding-ai-agents-in-biological-reality-building-blocks-for-autonomous-wet-labs/) (Google Research, blog, 2026-10-16): Google Research lays out the pieces needed for AI agents to run real lab experiments with robotics, and how it keeps agent plans tied to what actually happens at the bench.
- [OpenAI opens GPT-Rosalind to academic research labs](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2110841898076418336) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-15): A new academic plan puts OpenAI's life-sciences model in university labs, widening access beyond pharma partners.
- [OpenAI releases new mathematical results from an internal frontier model](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107596713791767021) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-06): Results were reviewed with an independent advisory group of mathematicians, a step toward credible AI contributions to research maths rather than benchmark claims.
