# AI turns to maths and science research

Frontier models are now producing research results, not just assisting: a Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis was certified correct in Lean, DeepMind's Gemini 4 agents found quantum circuits up to 10.9x cheaper to simulate, and an OpenAI preprint reports GPT-6 models designing proteins validated in the wet lab. Anthropic and OpenAI models have now each posted Lean-formalized claimed proofs that VP differs from VNP, though no human has checked either yet. Pairing models with formal or experimental verification is what makes these results credible, and research is becoming both a capability signal and a product line.

## Evidence

- [Anthropic and OpenAI models each post a Lean-checked proof that VP differs from VNP](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/blob/main/vp-vnp/README.md) (Anthropic, paper, 2026-11-27): Valiant's 1979 conjecture, the algebraic analogue of P vs NP, now has two AI-written claimed proofs with Lean formalizations. No human has checked either, and it does not settle P vs NP. OpenAI's batch also claims an answer to Smale's ninth problem.
- [OpenAI preprint: GPT-6 models design proteins validated in the wet lab](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities) (OpenAI, paper, 2026-11-24): The models design new protein shapes and sequences using reasoning and code alone, without calling specialised biology models, and the preprint reports wet-lab validation of the designs.
- [DeepMind's Gemini 4 agents find cheaper quantum circuits, proved correct in Lean](https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/) (Google DeepMind, paper, 2026-11-23): Agent teams worked from frozen specs and an external Lean checker, so gains could not come from weakened definitions. Up to 10.9x cheaper simulation circuits; code is open in google-deepmind/formal-quantum-computation.
- [Levent Alpöge's Claude-assisted proof of the Riemann Hypothesis is certified correct in Lean](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/) (Lean community, news, 2026-11-20): The 17-million-line Lean proof, built with Claude, was checked by Lean's kernel and several independent checkers. It is the clearest case yet of AI plus formal verification settling a major open problem.
- [OpenAI: agents now finish nearly half of 2-4 hour research tasks on their own](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/) (OpenAI, news, 2026-11-19): Autonomous success on 2-4h research tasks rose from 23% to 46% since March, and median researcher agent spend hit $2,400/day. OpenAI argues labs should be required to publicly report self-improvement indicators.
- [OpenAI models found up to 14% PPA gains on AMD's flagship GPU design](https://openai.com/index/advancing-chip-design-with-our-models) (OpenAI, news, 2026-11-18): Model rewrites of SystemVerilog RTL, verified with Synopsys tools, beat years of hand optimisation in under five months, and AMD has already sent some to manufacturing. A concrete case of frontier models doing expert hardware work, not just code.
- [GPT-6 Astra helps a physicist propose a lattice definition of the Standard Model](https://openai.com/index/step-toward-defining-the-standard-model) (OpenAI, news, 2026-11-15): UCSB's Nathaniel Craig says Astra helped build the construction and led the proof for a candidate nonperturbative definition, a 50-year-old open problem, now under expert review. It opens OpenAI's new science blog.
- [Google opens Antigravity's /teamwork multi-agent tools after results on open problems](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/) (Google Research, news, 2026-11-13): Google Research reports Gemini 4 multi-agent workflows producing results in math, formal verification, chemistry and kernel optimization, and is making /teamwork available to outside researchers.
- [DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2 predicts how DNA variants act in specific cell types](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120532824465805749) (x.com, news, 2026-11-11): It models how each cell type reads the same genome, so researchers can trace a genetic variant to the cells where a disease actually unfolds.
- [DeepMind's AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal's protocol implementation](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/) (Google DeepMind, news, 2026-11-10): Gemini-powered agents proved key security properties of Signal's compiled protocol code in four weeks, writing 6M+ lines of Lean with humans reviewing about 1%. A first for a widely deployed protocol's implementation; artifacts are open-sourced.
- [OpenAI introduces BioBasicsBench for AI agents on wet-lab analysis](https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science/) (OpenAI, news, 2026-11-06): 100 tasks from real Ligo experiments test whether agents can read raw gels, plates and instrument data, catch errors and refuse unsupported conclusions. GPT-6 Astra scores 66.7% at xhigh vs 43.8% for GPT-5.6 Sol.
- [Anthropic used Claude to help map the first 3D structure of a bacterial vault particle](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2118073665296228863) (Anthropic, news, 2026-11-04): A concrete case of a frontier model contributing to real structural biology work, from Anthropic's own research team.
- [Deep Principle releases Hydrogen-1.0, an open-weight science model post-trained from GLM-5.3](https://blog.deepprinciple.com/introducing-hydrogen-1.0/) (Deep Principle, release, 2026-11-02): MIT-licensed weights (1M context) for chemistry, materials and life sciences, trained on lab records and agent trajectories. Self-reported 38.3% on FrontierScience-Research vs GPT-6 Astra's 33.3%; not yet independently verified.
- [OpenAI and Anthropic models each claim a refutation of the Exponential Time Hypothesis](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/378.md) (OpenAI, paper, 2026-10-30): Both labs say internal models independently found a subexponential algorithm for counting 3-SAT solutions on Oct 16, with Lean proofs. Impagliazzo, Paturi and Williams are now leading an open review; the result is asymptotic only, with no practical speedup.
- [Physicists led by David Gross convene to vet an AI result on Yang-Mills theory](https://www.kitp.ucsb.edu/statement-by-the-yangmills-working-group) (KITP, news, 2026-10-28): KITP formed a working group chaired by Nobel laureate David Gross to vet an AI result that includes a proof that quantum Yang-Mills theory exists, half of a Millennium Prize problem. OpenAI amplified it, so physicists are now formally checking model-made frontier science.
- [Anthropic details its drug-discovery partnerships and starts its own drug research](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2115178675050307942) (Anthropic, news, 2026-10-27): Anthropic scientists spent months partnering with six drug developers and have started drug discovery of their own. The post shares where Claude actually helps across the stages of finding a new medicine.
- [Simons Foundation, OpenAI and Anthropic commit $15M to AI in maths research](https://x.com/SimonsFdn/status/2110740786169979088) (Simons Foundation, news, 2026-10-17): Workshops and free compute to help mathematicians verify and build on AI-generated results, after OpenAI's batch of 700 AI-written papers unsettled the field.
- [Claude Mythos 5 designs more efficient quantum circuits from plain-language instructions](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2111152183240511640) (Anthropic, paper, 2026-10-16): From Anthropic's STEM Fellows: a concrete case of a model doing useful design work in a specialised science domain.
- [Grounding AI agents in biological reality: building blocks for autonomous wet labs](https://research.google/blog/grounding-ai-agents-in-biological-reality-building-blocks-for-autonomous-wet-labs/) (Google Research, blog, 2026-10-16): Google Research lays out the pieces needed for AI agents to run real lab experiments with robotics, and how it keeps agent plans tied to what actually happens at the bench.
- [OpenAI opens GPT-Rosalind to academic research labs](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2110841898076418336) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-15): A new academic plan puts OpenAI's life-sciences model in university labs, widening access beyond pharma partners.
- [OpenAI releases new mathematical results from an internal frontier model](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107596713791767021) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-06): Results were reviewed with an independent advisory group of mathematicians, a step toward credible AI contributions to research maths rather than benchmark claims.
