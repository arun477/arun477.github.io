Personal agents get their own computers, accounts and money

Always-on personal agents went from demos to products this month. OpenAI launched dots with their own cloud computers, and Grok Bot added voice, finance, email, proactive suggestions, coding hand-offs and a presence in cars. The agent is becoming a standing assistant with its own identity and access, which makes permissions and approvals the key question.
