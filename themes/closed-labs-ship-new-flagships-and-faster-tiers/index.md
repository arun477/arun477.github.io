# Closed labs ship new flagships and faster tiers

The closed labs kept shipping: GPT-6 Sol, Luna and 6.1 Sol, Claude Haiku 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, Meta's Muse Wonder and now Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Carbon, which tops Artificial Analysis's new Index v5. OpenAI's GPT-6.1 Terra matches GPT-6 Astra on computer use at a tenth the price and over 3x the speed, aimed at high-volume agents. The competition is shifting toward speed, price tiers and agent workloads, and leaderboards are moving tests to private sets to keep rankings meaningful.

## Evidence

- [Google DeepMind announces Gemini 4 Carbon, its new frontier model](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-our-next-frontier-model/) (Google DeepMind, release, 2026-10-28): Two weeks after Argon, Carbon tops Artificial Analysis's new Index v5 at 59 and leads cyber benchmarks like SEC-bench Pro (88.2%). Access starts with vetted cyber defenders; wider developer release comes later at $2/$10 per million tokens.
- [Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 moves most of its tests to private sets](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2115265133467635877) (Artificial Analysis, news, 2026-10-28): The most-cited model leaderboard now scores mostly on private, held-out data, with new agentic coding and terminal benchmarks, which makes benchmark gaming harder. Expect labs to quote v5 numbers in upcoming launches.
- [OpenAI releases GPT-6.1 Terra, a cheaper, faster model for computer use](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-26): Terra matches GPT-6 Astra on OpenAI's computer-use benchmark at a tenth the price per token and runs over 3x faster, with API prices half of GPT-6.1 Sol. Built for high-volume screen-driving agents; live in the API, ChatGPT and Codex.
- [OpenAI open-sources MultiLangBench and reports big gains on COBOL and Fortran](https://openai.com/index/low-resource-programming-languages/) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-20): GPT-6 Astra passes 87% of a build-SQLite-from-scratch test across 20 languages (GPT-5.6 Sol: 27%), and GPT-6.1 Sol hits 98% on COBOL migration. The eval suite is public, so you can check your own language.
- [Google releases Gemini 4 Argon globally](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2110039274922131792) (Google DeepMind, release, 2026-10-13): Google's new frontier model for coding, finance, legal and security work. Cursor measures it on par with Opus 5.5 at $2/$10 per million tokens.
- [Meta introduces Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2109691148943999167) (AI at Meta, release, 2026-10-12): Meta Superintelligence Labs' first frontier models, 14 months after its rebuild, aimed at personal and professional agentic tasks.
- [OpenAI rolls out Ultrafast mode for GPT-6.1 Sol](https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/2108262812489531498) (OpenAI Developers, release, 2026-10-08): Up to 8x faster than standard Sol across the API, Codex and ChatGPT Work, making near-top-tier models practical for interactive agent loops.
- [Anthropic releases Claude Haiku 5.5](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2107894039626277339) (Claude, release, 2026-10-07): About 75% cheaper to run than Haiku 4.5 and Anthropic's most capable small model, which changes the cost of high-volume agent subtasks.
- [GPT-6 and Intelligent UI roll out to everyone in ChatGPT](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2107894997538525580) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-07): Free users get GPT-6, and answers can now come back as generated interactive interfaces instead of only text.
- [GPT-6.1 Sol offers near-Astra intelligence at a fifth of the price](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2104986129686741046) (OpenAI, release, 2026-09-29): OpenAI's new cost-efficient model narrows the gap between flagship quality and price, which matters most for agents that run all day.
- [OpenAI releases GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2102460975790137662) (OpenAI, release, 2026-09-22): Two faster, cheaper models carry most of GPT-6 Astra's strengths. They're aimed at running agents and high-volume work at lower cost.
