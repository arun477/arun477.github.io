# Anthropic reshapes its governance on the way to an IPO

Anthropic filed an S-1 to list on Nasdaq as ANT, a week after committing 5% of its shares to a Public Benefit Fund that, with staff pledges, puts about 20% of its equity toward public benefit. It also handed its Agent Skills standard to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation, as it did with MCP. A frontier lab going public puts its finances and governance in the open and sets a template for how safety-focused labs raise capital.

## Evidence

- [Anthropic files its S-1 for an IPO on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec) (Anthropic, news, 2026-10-26): Anthropic filed with the SEC for a proposed IPO of its Class A stock on Nasdaq, led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley. Share count and price aren't set yet, but going public puts a frontier lab's finances and governance in the open.
- [Anthropic hands Agent Skills to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation) (Anthropic, news, 2026-10-23): The SKILL.md folder standard, already in 70+ agents including ChatGPT, Cursor and Copilot, moves to neutral governance like MCP did, with a roadmap for versioned releases and security guidance for installing skills.
- [Anthropic commits 5% of its shares to a new Public Benefit Fund](https://www.anthropic.com/news/public-benefit-fund) (Anthropic, news, 2026-10-18): Subject to stockholder approval, the stake can grow 2% for every 50% rise in value; with founder and staff pledges, about 20% of Anthropic's equity is now committed to public benefit. AI's labour-market impact is a named focus, and none of it goes to lobbying.
