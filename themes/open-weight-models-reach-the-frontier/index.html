Open-weight models reach the frontier

Mistral Large 4, Meta's Muse Spark Open and Reflection's Beam put frontier-scale models under open licences within two weeks. Vercel's gateway data shows open weights already carry most production tokens. Self-hosting is now a real alternative to paid APIs for coding and agent work.

6 Oct – 17 Oct Feed

Sat 17 October 2026

  1. SpikeAI at MetaNewsModels17:08 UTCMeta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B open-weight model for coding and agents816B total, 42B active, 1M-token context. Artificial Analysis ranks it the top US open-weight model, though the licence requires attribution and a deal for providers over $20M.↗
  2. ReflectionNewsOpen sourceReflection releases Beam with open weights under Apache 2.0A second large open model in a day, but with a fully permissive licence: weights, tokenizer, reference code and FP8/NVFP4 builds, so teams can self-host and ship commercially with no strings.↗
