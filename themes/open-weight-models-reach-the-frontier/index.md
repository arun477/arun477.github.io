# Open-weight models reach the frontier

Frontier-class open weights keep arriving at every size: Sber released GigaChat 4, a 708B MoE with 1M context, Tencent put Hy4 flash on Hugging Face, Baidu opened ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview under Apache 2.0, and Sarvam shipped a 105B checkpoint tuned for agents and Indian languages. The serving stack is maturing alongside: llama.cpp reached 1.0, Tencent open-sourced the DFly2 speculative drafter for sparse-attention models, and a coding agent tuned Inkling 2 Mini serving on a single workstation GPU. Open release is a normal path to strong capability, and running it on your own hardware keeps getting more practical.

## Evidence

- [Sber releases GigaChat 4 open weights: 708B-A41B MoE with 1M context](https://huggingface.co/ai-sage/GigaChat4-708B-A41B) (Sber (Hugging Face), release, 2026-11-26): A new large open-weight model outside the US and China, mixing MoE with DeepSeek Sparse Attention and Gated DeltaNet, with instant and reasoning modes in one checkpoint. FP8, BF16, base and GGUF weights are all published.
- [Tencent open-sources DFly2, a speculative drafter for sparse-attention models](https://github.com/Tencent/AngelSpec) (Tencent Hunyuan, release, 2026-11-26): The drafter reuses the target model's sparse KV cache, so long contexts stay cheap: up to 81% more serving throughput for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at 64K. Training code and drafters for Hy4 flash, DeepSeek and GLM flash models are out.
- [A coding agent hill-climbs Inkling 2 Mini serving on one RTX PRO 6000](https://huggingface.co/blog/inkling-2-mini-inference-hillclimb) (Hugging Face, blog, 2026-11-25): An agent with a control-bracketed benchmark protocol took single-user decode from 136 to 190 tok/s via vLLM nightly plus DSpark speculation, near the bandwidth ceiling, and logs what failed and why.
- [Baidu open-sources ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview weights under Apache 2.0](https://huggingface.co/baidu/ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Preview) (Baidu, release, 2026-11-24): A 280B-total, 14B-active MoE with 1M-token context, previously API-only, now downloadable with day-0 SGLang support. Baidu says it was trending toward the #2 open model in Code Arena WebDev.
- [Sarvam releases Sarvam-105B-1711, an Apache-2.0 open-weight model tuned for agents and Indian languages](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/sarvam-105b-1711) (Sarvam AI, release, 2026-11-22): A new checkpoint of the 105B MoE (10.3B active) with big tool-use and instruction-following gains, a 4-bit build that fits one workstation GPU, and notes on doubling training throughput on Blackwell. Benchmarks are Sarvam's own.
- [Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Mini, a 26B looped open-weights model for laptops](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-mini/) (Thinking Machines, release, 2026-11-20): A 26B MoE (3.4B active) that reruns its middle layers three times per token; it leads open models under 100B on DeepSWE and comes with research on how looping affects chain-of-thought monitoring.
- [Tencent opens the weights of Hy4 flash, a 224B MoE with 12B active and 1M context](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/2122912575302119554) (Tencent Hunyuan, release, 2026-11-18): Two days after the cheap API launch, Tencent put Hy4 flash, an FP8 build and the base model on Hugging Face. A natively multimodal 1M-context model you can self-host is a strong new option for open-weight agent work, though 224B total parameters still needs a multi-GPU node.
- [Hugging Face: running a 180B MoE in 32GB of RAM by streaming experts from SSD](https://huggingface.co/blog/moe-expert-streaming) (Hugging Face, blog, 2026-11-18): A hands-on account of offloading MoE experts to disk in Transformers to run 121GB of experts on a 32GB Mac, getting within 6% of a dedicated engine on token generation. Shows what local inference of big open models now costs.
- [How Thinking Machines trained Inkling 2 Small end to end in 4-bit](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/end-to-end-4-bit-training) (Thinking Machines, blog, 2026-11-13): A detailed account of using NVFP4 in the forward pass from the first pretraining step through RL, and how they kept quantization error from hurting learning.
- [llama.cpp and ggml reach v1.0.0 with a stable API and ABI](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1) (Hugging Face, release, 2026-11-13): After 3.5 years, the core local-inference library commits to semantic versioning, so apps built on it can upgrade without surprise breakage.
- [Thinking Machines opens Inkling 2 Small weights under Apache 2.0](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2120982625582195011) (Thinking Machines, release, 2026-11-12): The 594B-total, 17B-active MoE for agents and coding, launched last week, can now be downloaded from Hugging Face under a permissive Apache 2.0 license, so teams can self-host and fine-tune it.
- [Runway releases Praxis-1, an open-weight world action model for robots](https://x.com/runwayml/status/2120533081182577116) (Runway, release, 2026-11-11): Apache 2.0 weights for a model that turns video pretraining into robot control, ranked first on NVIDIA's RoboLab-120. A strong open base for teams building robot agents.
- [Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small, a 594B/17B MoE for agents and coding](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/) (Thinking Machines, release, 2026-11-05): Second-generation Inkling scores 31 on the AA Intelligence Index; available now on Tinker for inference and fine-tuning, with Apache 2.0 weights planned next week after safety checks.
- [Mistral releases open weights for Le Chonk (Mistral Large 4), a 1T-parameter MoE for coding agents and cyber defense](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2115811242556137820) (Mistral AI, release, 2026-10-29): Updated Mistral Large 4 weights (about 1T total, 49B active parameters, 1M context) ship in FP8 and NVFP4, and Mistral says it tops open models on Cognition's FrontierCode. It's a frontier-class model teams can self-host for coding agents.
- [Moonshot releases Kimi K3.1 Pro, a 2.8T-parameter open-weight model](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2113680701770068067) (Moonshot AI, release, 2026-10-23): An open-weight MoE with 104B active parameters, 1M-token context and multimodal input, pitched at full-project coding. It landed in Vercel AI Gateway and OpenCode the same day, so it is usable right away.
- [DeepSeek open-sources DeepInfer, the inference stack behind V4.1-Flash](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepInfer) (DeepSeek, release, 2026-10-20): DeepSeek's production serving engine is now Apache-2.0, with expert parallelism, draft-token decoding and deterministic sampling on Nvidia SM100 and Huawei Ascend 950. KV cache and disaggregation are not included.
- [MiniMax M3.1 Flash: the stealth 'Space Bunny' model goes official](https://x.com/MiniMaxAgent/status/2112155992708538688) (MiniMax, release, 2026-10-19): MiniMax's new open-weight coding and agent model has a 1M-token context and already topped OpenCode for 12 days as the anonymous 'Space Bunny', serving 106T tokens. It is free on OpenCode and Vercel AI Gateway for a few days.
- [Meta publishes the Muse Glimmer 30B technical report](https://ai.meta.com/research/publications/muse-glimmer-30b-an-on-device-agentic-model/) (AI at Meta, paper, 2026-10-19): The first full write-up of Meta's Apache 2.0 local agent model: architecture, distillation recipe, 4-bit quantization and training in simulated multi-day digital lives. A rare detailed recipe for agents that run on one consumer GPU.
- [Meta releases Muse Spark Open, an 816B open-weight model for coding and agents](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2110779754274292212) (AI at Meta, news, 2026-10-17): 816B total, 42B active, 1M-token context. Artificial Analysis ranks it the top US open-weight model, though the licence requires attribution and a deal for providers over $20M.
- [Reflection releases Beam with open weights under Apache 2.0](https://x.com/reflection_ai/status/2111079965529968798) (Reflection, news, 2026-10-17): A second large open model in a day, but with a fully permissive licence: weights, tokenizer, reference code and FP8/NVFP4 builds, so teams can self-host and ship commercially with no strings.
- [DeepSeek ships V4.1-Flash-1017, a faster, cheaper open-weight update](https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2111375640717033777) (x.com, release, 2026-10-17): DeepSeek's refreshed Flash model posts stronger benchmark scores with shorter outputs and faster task completion, and is live on its app and API. It is tuned for agent harnesses including DeepSeek Harness and Tencent CodeBuddy.
- [Open weight takes 71.5% of tokens and 23% of spend](https://vercel.com/blog/ai-gateway-production-index-october-2026) (Vercel, blog, 2026-10-15): Production data from Vercel's AI Gateway for September: open-weight models handled over 70% of tokens, AI spend grew 13x and cost per token halved.
- [Qwen releases Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo with open weights](https://x.com/Alibaba_Qwen/status/2108549075218120949) (Qwen, release, 2026-10-09): Generates and edits images in 8 denoising steps, making high-quality open image models much cheaper to run.
- [Mistral releases Large 4, a 1T-parameter open-weight multimodal model](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2107457414387622310) (Mistral AI, release, 2026-10-06): 1T total, 49B active, natively multimodal, and Mistral says it leads open models from the US or Europe on aggregate benchmarks. Europe's biggest open release yet.
- [Google DeepMind releases EmbeddingGemma 2 for on-device multimodal embeddings](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2107502286758895878) (Google DeepMind, release, 2026-10-06): One small open model embeds text, code, images, audio and video in a shared space, useful for local retrieval in agents without a server.
