# Assistants move into the tools people already use

Assistants keep spreading into surfaces people already live in: Stripe now lets any business query payments data through an agent via MCP, and Meta rolled out Muse Voice and Realtime Avatar across mobile, web and Mac. Muse Spaces goes further, letting people describe a planner, tracker or hub and get a persistent mini-app Muse builds and keeps updated in the background. That sits alongside Claude in Google Docs and Sheets, live dashboards in Claude, and Cursor bundled into familiar subscriptions. Distribution through existing tools remains as important as raw model quality.

## Evidence

- [Meta launches Spaces: custom mini-apps Muse builds from a description](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-spaces-customize-muse-app/) (Meta, news, 2026-11-06): Describe a planner, tracker or hub and Muse builds it into the app, with tabs you can pin or schedule and connected apps that keep it updated in the background. Available everywhere Muse is.
- [Stripe lets any business analyze payments data through an agent via MCP](https://stripe.com/blog/analyze-your-stripe-data-directly-with-your-agent) (stripe.com, news, 2026-11-05): Natural-language queries over Stripe data for reports, fraud and retention metrics are generally available; agents connect through the Stripe MCP or Queries API.
- [Meta rolls out Muse Voice and Realtime Avatar in the US](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/11/introducing-muse-ai-voice-expressive-avatars/) (about.fb.com, news, 2026-11-05): US users can talk to Muse in real time on mobile, web and Mac, with expressive avatars; voice is coming next to AI Glasses and Muse Charm.
- [OpenAI upgrades ChatGPT Voice with GPT-Live-1.1](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2110797830361436604) (OpenAI, news, 2026-10-17): New voices and accents, including Hindi, and less stalling, rolling out to all users with API access next. Part of a wider voice race that also saw Grok Voice 3.0 top a reliability benchmark.
- [Cursor and Grok join a bundled X Subscription](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2109783879490191502) (Cursor, news, 2026-10-12): Grok, Cursor and X Premium now share one plan and usage pool from $8 a month, a notable bundling of a coding agent with a consumer assistant.
- [Claude adds live dashboards and animated explainers in beta](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2108271552991252810) (Claude, news, 2026-10-08): Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion turn data and ideas into live, shareable artifacts, pushing chat assistants toward building small apps.
- [Claude now works inside Google Docs, Sheets and Slides](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2107522596845822135) (Claude, news, 2026-10-06): A sidebar that reads and edits the open file puts Claude directly into Google's own suite, competing with Gemini on its home turf.
- [ChatGPT Voice can now use email, calendar and Slack plugins](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2102808325742322002) (OpenAI, release, 2026-09-23): Voice in ChatGPT can now act through your connected apps and runs on the GPT-6 models. Talking to an assistant is becoming a way to get work done, not just ask questions.
